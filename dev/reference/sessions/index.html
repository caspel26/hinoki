hinoki.sessions.MemorySessionStore ¶

MemorySessionStore(max_sessions: int = 10000)

Bases: SessionStore

Bounded, process-local store for tests and single-worker deployments.

Revoked and idle-expired sessions are removed at once, so they neither keep their data nor count against max_sessions. Their token digests are kept until the session's absolute expiry (at most max_sessions of them, the oldest dropped first) so a request that loaded one before it was revoked cannot save it back.
